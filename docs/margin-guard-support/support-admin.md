# Support Inbox

Support Inbox is a **platform-administrator** view of confirmed Margin Guard Support cases. It is not a customer help desk and not a tenant-owner ticket list.

## Who can use it

Only Margin Guard platform administrators can open `/support-admin`. Normal owners, sellers, supervisors, and device sessions cannot list or update cases here.

## What you can do

- Review open, resolved, or all cases
- See limited operational context: case reference, business name, subject, category, module, page, and a sanitized question excerpt
- **Mark resolved** on an open case
- **Reopen case** on a resolved case

## What this inbox does not include

- Tenant-owner “my tickets” history
- Customer or signer personal data
- Payments, invoices amounts, contract values, or other financial data
- Assignment, priority, due-date targets, internal notes, chat, email, or attachments
- Automatic notifications

A case is created only after an owner confirms **Create support case** in Ask Margin Guard. This inbox reviews those confirmed cases. It does not create cases.
